OpenConnect — One Platform. All Payments.

What is OpenConnect?​

OpenConnect is your enterprise payment powerhouse. One unified platform that connects to all payment networks—enabling instant payments, bill settlements, bulk disbursements, and merchant transactions through a single, easy-to-integrate API.

Think of it as: Your payment gateway that speaks every language, connects to every network, and guarantees every transaction gets delivered.

What You Can Do With OpenConnect​

Person-to-Person (P2P) – Instant transfers via mobile, CNIC, email, or account
Person-to-Merchant (P2M) – QR codes, NFC, Request-to-Pay
Bill Payments – Utilities, telecom, government, aggregators
Bulk Payouts – Salary, subsidies, refunds, corporate payments
Zero Transaction Loss – Guaranteed delivery, automatic retries
Complete Visibility – Real-time monitoring, transaction tracing, reporting

OpenConnect in 60 Seconds​

Customer → App → OpenConnect → Smart Routing → Payment Network → Done

How it works:

  1. Customer initiates payment
  2. OpenConnect validates and applies business rules
  3. Automatically routes to best network (RAAST, 1LINK, etc.)
  4. Network processes transaction
  5. Confirmation delivered in seconds
  6. Full audit trail captured
